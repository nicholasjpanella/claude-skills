The page adds no facts that aren't in the brief, including descriptive copy: what a service includes, named jobs, parts or systems (e.g. tubeless, bleeds, tyre replacement, motor or battery), brands, policies (parts extra, call before work, we'll confirm a slot), turnaround, guarantees, contact details, example figures, testimonials. Anything like that is a visible placeholder on the page and listed in the final message. A line that only restates a listed service as the customer's problem ("Flat tyre? Bring it in.", "Gears slipping or skipping") is allowed and doesn't count as an added fact
