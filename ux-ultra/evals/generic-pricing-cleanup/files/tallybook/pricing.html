FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I bill by word count?

Yes. Set a rate per source word, per target word, per page or per hour for each client, and Tallybook works out the line totals from the counts you enter.

Which currencies can I invoice in?

Any of the 30 currencies the European Central Bank publishes daily rates for. Starter invoices in one currency; Pro and Agency can mix them.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You keep access until the end of the month you've paid for, and you can export every invoice as PDF and CSV first.
